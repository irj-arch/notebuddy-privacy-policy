Privacy Policy for NoteBuddy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

NoteBuddy is a note-taking and productivity application. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use its features.

2. Information and Local Storage

NoteBuddy is designed to store notes and other supported content locally on your device. The app does not use Firebase or a developer-operated cloud service to store or synchronize your notes.

3. Voice Features

NoteBuddy supports voice-related functionality, such as voice recording or voice-to-text. Audio may be processed on your device or through an external speech-processing service, depending on the implementation and device. If an external service is used, its privacy practices may apply.

4. Permissions

The app may request microphone permission for voice features, notification permission for reminders, and access to images or files that you choose to attach. Actual permissions depend on the installed version.

5. Analytics and Advertising

Based on the current developer configuration, NoteBuddy does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Device operating systems and other services may independently process technical information under their own policies.

6. Data Sharing

NoteBuddy does not upload locally stored notes to a developer-operated cloud service. Information may be processed by your device or an external service when you choose a feature that requires it, including voice-to-text.

7. Security and Retention

Locally stored information may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its data, reset your device, or experience device failure. Keep backups of important content where possible.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information through the app's local note-storage functionality. Applicable children's privacy requirements may apply depending on the app's audience and use of external services.

9. Your Choices

You can manage your notes using the app's available features and control device permissions through your device settings. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored content.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
